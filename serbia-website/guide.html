Travel Essentials

Your Guide to Serbia

🛂 Visa & Entry

▼
  • Visa-Free: Chinese citizens holding ordinary passports valid for at least 6 months are eligible for visa-free entry for up to 30 days
  • No Prior Application: No invitation letter required
  • On Arrival: Simply complete an arrival card upon entry
  • Passport Validity: At least 6 months beyond your planned stay

🚄 Transportation

▼
  • Beijing–Belgrade: Hainan Airlines and Air Serbia operate direct flights (~10-12 hours)
  • Belgrade–Novi Sad: High-speed railway in just 35 minutes, tickets ~20 RMB
  • Local Transport: Buses, trams, and taxis readily available
  • Rental Cars: Available at airports and major cities

💱 Currency & Costs

▼
  • Currency: Serbian Dinar (RSD)
  • Exchange Rate: 1 CNY ≈ 20 RSD
  • Daily Budget: 200-300 RMB per person per day
  • Payment: Credit cards accepted widely; carry some cash for smaller places

Important Information

Safety & Etiquette

✅

Safety: Serbia is one of the safest countries in Europe with excellent public security. Violent crime is rare, and tourists are generally well-treated.

⛪

Church Etiquette: Dress modestly when entering churches — avoid shorts and short skirts. Remove hats when inside Orthodox churches.

💬

Conversation: Please refrain from discussing the Kosovo issue. Serbians are generally open and friendly — talk about culture, food, and football instead!

🤝

Hospitality: If invited to someone's home, bring a small gift (flowers, sweets, or wine) as a gesture of appreciation.
