Introduction

The Unique Charm of the East-West Crossroads

Located in the heart of the Balkan Peninsula, Serbia is a true "East-West crossroads." Throughout history, this land has been the meeting point of the Roman Empire, Byzantine Empire, Ottoman Empire, and Austro-Hungarian Empire. The fusion of cultures has left behind a rich historical legacy and a distinctive character.

From ancient fortresses to untouched forests, from energetic cities to peaceful riverside cabins, Serbia perfectly blends European elegance with Balkan wilderness. As an important node of China's Belt and Road Initiative, Serbia offers 30-day visa-free entry for Chinese citizens, making travel more convenient than ever.

Whether you want to explore medieval fortresses, wander through primeval forests, or enjoy authentic čevapčići and rakija, Serbia promises an unforgettable immersive experience.

Seasons

Best Time to Visit

🌸

Spring

April – June. Pleasant 15-25°C. Cherry blossoms in Belgrade, green hills of Tara, and fewer crowds. Perfect for hiking and city walks.

☀️

Summer

June – August. Warm 25-35°C. EXIT Festival, river swimming at Ada Ciganlija, and Danube cruises. Peak season with vibrant nightlife.

🍂

Autumn

September – November. Crisp 10-20°C. Wine harvest in Fruška Gora, golden forests in Tara, and Guča Trumpet Festival. Best for photography.

❄️

Winter

December – March. Cold -5 to 5°C. Skiing at Kopaonik and Zlatibor, Belgrade's cozy kafanas, and traditional Slava celebrations. A magical winter wonderland.
