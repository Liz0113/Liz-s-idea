Destinations

City Pulse

Belgrade — Gateway to the Balkans

The thousand-year-old capital where the Danube and Sava rivers meet

Historic Fortress

Kalemegdan Fortress

Offering stunning views where the Danube and Sava rivers meet. The ancient walls have witnessed centuries of history — every stone tells a story of empires risen and fallen.

🏛️ Historic🌅 Panoramic Views
Bohemian Quarter

Skadarlija District

In the evening, explore the bohemian quarter of Skadarlija, perfect for authentic food and live music. This cobblestone street is Belgrade's Montmartre.

🎵 Live Music🍽️ Traditional Food

Novi Sad — Serbia's Cultural Heart

Youthful energy meets artistic tradition

Iconic Fortress

Petrovaradin Fortress

The city's landmark and host of the world-famous EXIT Music Festival every summer. Stroll along the Danube and feel the youthful, artistic vibe of this university town.

🎶 EXIT Festival🏰 Fortress
Music Festival

EXIT Music Festival

One of Europe's top music festivals, held every July at Petrovaradin Fortress. 40+ stages with electronic, rock, pop, and global music, lasting late into the night.

🎵 40+ Stages🌙 Night Sessions

Nature & Landscapes

Wild Beauty

House on the Drina — Symbol of Serbia

A tiny wooden cabin standing alone on a rock in the middle of a river

National Park

Tara National Park

One of Serbia's most beautiful national parks. The iconic House on the Drina has become the symbol of Serbia. Dense primeval forests and rich wildlife — ideal for hiking, photography, and reconnecting with nature.

🌲 Primeval Forests📸 Photography🥾 Hiking
Natural Wonder

Uvac Canyon

Famous for spectacular U-shaped meanders, often called one of the most beautiful canyons in Europe. A paradise for rare griffon vultures. Take a boat ride or hot-air balloon to admire breathtaking views.

🦅 Vultures🚣 Boat Ride🎈 Balloon

Hidden Gems

Secret Places

Drvengrad (Wooden Town)

Zlatibor Region

Built by renowned director Emir Kusturica as the set for his film Life Is a Miracle, this fairy-tale wooden village has become a living open-air museum. With charming wooden houses, cafés, and even a cinema, it offers a magical blend of art, folklore, and creativity.

🎬 Kusturica🪵 Wooden Architecture📸 Photography

Travel Tips

Getting Around

🚄

High-Speed Rail

Belgrade to Novi Sad in just 35 minutes. Tickets cost around 20 RMB. Comfortable, modern trains with Wi-Fi.

🚗

Rent a Car

For Tara, Uvac, Drvengrad — a car is recommended. Daily rates ~200-300 RMB. Roads are good, scenery is spectacular.

🚌

Bus Network

Extensive bus network connects all major cities and towns. Affordable (10-50 RMB per trip), reliable, and frequent.
